Export to CSV

Export your processed emails to a CSV file for use in spreadsheet applications or for backup purposes.

The CSV will include the following fields:

  • Sender information
  • Email subject
  • Date received
  • Email status (read/unread)

  • Complete email data including HTML and text versions
  • User account information
  • Progress tracking data

Data Management

About Your Data

Your email data is stored securely in a SQLite database on the server. You can export your data at any time using the options above.

The system processes your emails to clean HTML content and organize the data for easier access and analysis. No permanent changes are made to your original emails in your Google account.

Privacy & Security

Your data is stored only for your use and is not shared with third parties.

You can delete all your data by using the "Reset Progress" option in your profile settings.
